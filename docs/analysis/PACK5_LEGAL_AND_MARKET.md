## 6. Three Structured Interview Scripts

### Script 1: Independent Travel Agents & Corporate Travel Desks
| Question # | Exact Interview Question | Target Insight / Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | *"How many hotel quotes does your team prepare each week for corporate or leisure clients, and roughly how much time do you spend clicking through OTAs to get the final price with taxes and fees?"* | Quantify weekly labor hours lost to manual checkout checking. |
| Q2 | *"How often does a client come back saying they found a cheaper price on Agoda or Cleartrip, only for you to discover it was a misleading base rate before convenience fees or taxes?"* | Validate emotional pain of client trust erosion due to drip pricing. |
| Q3 | *"What tools do you currently use to cross-check rates across multiple portals, and where do they fall short?"* | Identify competitor penetration (manual tabs vs Google Hotels). |
| Q4 | *"If you could generate a verified, all-in quote across 3 OTAs with a timestamped proof receipt in 10 seconds, how would that change your workflow?"* | Assess perceived value of instant verified quotes and proof receipts. |
| Q5 | *"Would your agency pay ₹2,500 to ₹5,000/month (or ₹15–₹25 per verified quote) for a tool that automates this and provides branded proof sheets for your clients?"* | **PILOT GATE QUESTION:** Direct commitment to paid pilot. |

### Script 2: Hotel Revenue Managers & General Managers
| Question # | Exact Interview Question | Target Insight / Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | *"How do you currently detect when OTAs (like Agoda, Booking.com, or MMT) undercut your direct brand rate through hidden discounts, closed-user-group coupons, or wholesale rate leaks?"* | Uncover current detection cadence and blind spots. |
| Q2 | *"When you dispute a rate parity violation with an OTA market manager, what evidence do they demand before correcting the rate (e.g. screenshots, final checkout screen, timestamps)?"* | Establish minimum evidence requirements for dispute resolution. |
| Q3 | *"Existing tools like StayParity or RateGain scrape public listing pages. How often does an OTA pass parity on the search page but apply an unauthorized discount at the final checkout step?"* | Validate checkout-level parity failure mode that scrapers miss. |
| Q4 | *"Would you pay ₹8,000 to ₹15,000/month for an automated monitor that walks full guest checkouts and delivers cryptographically signed, timestamped proof of checkout-level parity violations?"* | **PILOT GATE QUESTION:** Validate hotelier willingness to pay. |

### Script 3: Travel Tech / OTA QA, Compliance & Engineering Teams
| Question # | Exact Interview Question | Target Insight / Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | *"How does your compliance team verify that your checkout flow complies with consumer protection guidelines regarding upfront fee disclosure and drip pricing?"* | Audit exposure to CCPA / FTC regulatory enforcement. |
| Q2 | *"How do you monitor competitor checkout fee additions (convenience fees, payment gateway surcharges, dynamic markups) on an ongoing basis?"* | Benchmark competitor intelligence operations. |
| Q3 | *"How many engineering hours per sprint are spent maintaining synthetic checkout monitoring scripts that break on UI or bot-detection changes?"* | Quantify synthetic monitoring maintenance costs. |
| Q4 | *"Would your team subscribe to an enterprise API feed providing daily verified checkout fee breakdowns and compliance audit trails across Indian and global OTAs?"* | **PILOT GATE QUESTION:** Validate enterprise API subscription demand. |

## 7. The Validation Pilot Gate

| Gate Parameter | Threshold | Decision Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid Pilot Conversion Gate** | **$\ge 3$ of 10 interviewees** | If $\ge 3$ out of 10 interviewees commit to a paid pilot (e.g. ₹2,500–₹5,000/mo), proceed to engineer the B2B portal. |
| **Pivot Protocol** | **$< 3$ of 10 interviewees** | If $<3$ commit, abort B2B custom software engineering; pivot primary focus to consumer browser extension and affiliate monetization. |
